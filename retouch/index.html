About

Born in São Paulo, graduated in Architecture in 2008.

Alongside architectural photography, I’ve worked as a retoucher since 2016: first leading the retouching team at Global Brands Group in London, then freelance for automotive and fashion campaigns. Since 2025, generative AI has been part of my everyday workflow.

I work between Lisbon and São Paulo and I’m available for projects anywhere.
